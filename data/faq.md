# Kathara salon, demo information
Hours: open every day from 11:00 to 21:00.
Booking: we recommend booking ahead. Walk ins are welcome when a stylist is free.
Colour and chemical treatments: a patch test is recommended and these take longer.
Cancellation: please cancel or reschedule at least 3 hours before your slot.
Late arrival: after 15 minutes we may need to shorten or reschedule the service.
Payment: cash, UPI and cards accepted.
Prices depend on the stylist tier and hair length.
